Track every job application in one place

JobPipe is a free, no-signup, local-first tracker for job seekers and freelancers. One clean board for every application, proposal, and follow-up — with nothing stored on a server.

Open the app — free, no sign-up

Works in your browser. Your data stays on your device.

Features

Kanban board

Five stages: Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected. Drag cards between stages, reorder within a stage, and move cards by keyboard or status menu.

Proposal templates

Save reusable proposals with placeholders for company, role, name, and skills. Fill one from any job and copy it to your clipboard.

Follow-up reminders

Set a follow-up date on any application. Overdue and due-today follow-ups appear at the top of the board, with an optional browser notification.

Statistics

See applications per week, response rate, and interview rate, plus counts by stage — drawn as lightweight charts with no chart library.

Export and import

Back up everything as JSON and restore it later. Imports are validated and ask for confirmation before replacing your data.

Offline, installable

JobPipe is a progressive web app. After the first load it works offline, and you can install it on your phone or computer.

How it works

  1. Open the app. No account, no sign-up, no install required to start.
  2. Add an application. Company, role, platform, rate, location, dates, tags, and contact — as much or as little as you have.
  3. Move it along. Drag it from Saved to Applied to Interview to Offer, set a follow-up, and reuse a proposal template for the next one.

Privacy, by design

JobPipe has no backend. Your applications, templates, and settings are stored only in your browser's IndexedDB, on your own device. There is no account to create, no server that receives your data, no analytics, and no tracking. If you want a backup, you export a JSON file yourself — nobody else gets a copy. Frequently asked questions

Is JobPipe really free?

Yes. JobPipe is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no paid plan, no premium tier, and no feature locked behind payment.

Do I need to sign up to use JobPipe?

No. JobPipe has no accounts and no sign-up. Open the app and start tracking applications immediately.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser, in IndexedDB on your own device. JobPipe has no server and no database of its own, so your applications are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Does JobPipe track me?

No. JobPipe has no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers. The only storage it uses is your own browser storage for your applications, templates, and theme preference.

Can I use JobPipe offline?

Yes. JobPipe is a progressive web app. After the first load, the app works offline because your data and the app itself live in your browser.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Clearing browser data deletes your JobPipe applications, because they live only in that browser. Export a JSON backup regularly from inside the app, and import it to restore your data.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export your applications and templates as a JSON file at any time, and import a previously exported file back into JobPipe. Import is validated and asks for confirmation before it replaces your current data.
