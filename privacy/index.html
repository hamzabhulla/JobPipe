Privacy

Short version: JobPipe has no server, no accounts, and no analytics. Your data stays in your browser.

What JobPipe stores

JobPipe stores your job applications, proposal templates, and settings (theme and notification preference) in your browser's IndexedDB, on your own device. That storage is created by the app running in your browser — it is not sent anywhere.

What JobPipe does not collect

  • No account, email address, name, or password is ever asked for or stored by JobPipe.
  • No analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party trackers are used.
  • No application data is sent to a server, because there is no server.

Export and import

When you export, your browser downloads a JSON file to your device. When you import, your browser reads a file you chose. In both cases the file moves only between your browser and your device.

Notifications

Browser notifications for follow-ups only work if you grant permission in your browser, and they are generated locally from your own follow-up dates. Declining permission does not remove the in-app follow-up panel.

Clearing your data

Clearing your browser's site data for JobPipe deletes your applications, templates, and settings. Export a JSON backup regularly if you want to keep a copy outside the browser.
