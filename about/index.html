About JobPipe

Freelancers and remote-job seekers often juggle dozens of applications across LinkedIn, Upwork, Fiverr, and email. Spreadsheets get stale, paid trackers need an account and hold your data on someone else's server. JobPipe is the middle path: a single board, in your browser, with nothing to sign up for.

How it works

Open the app, add an application, and drag it through Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, and Rejected. Add a follow-up date, reuse a proposal template, check your weekly stats, and export a JSON backup whenever you like. Everything runs in the browser with React, TypeScript, and IndexedDB (via Dexie.js). There is no backend.

What it is not

JobPipe does not find jobs for you, does not apply on your behalf, and does not sync between devices. Those are deliberate limits of a local-first tool, not missing features waiting for a paywall — see the roadmap in the README for what is planned, including optional cloud sync with a private key.
